Our Story

Design that gets built properly

RP Studio is an interior design studio in Girgaon, Mumbai, led by Rahul. We work with families and businesses across Mumbai and nearby areas, turning ideas and concepts into well finished interior spaces.

Many designers hand your drawings to a contractor and step away. We stay with the job. Our team handles the design as well as the civil, electrical, plumbing, ceiling, furniture and painting work, so the result matches the plan.
